Our Story

Slow fashion, built on three promises.

We started Velmora with a simple frustration: good clothes shouldn’t fall apart, and honest clothes shouldn’t cost a fortune. So we cut out the middlemen, partnered directly with family-run mills, and publish the true cost of every garment we make.

  • Traceable. Every piece lists its mill, its maker, and its journey.
  • Durable. Reinforced seams and natural fibres, guaranteed for five years.
  • Circular. Send any Velmora piece back — we repair it or recycle it, free.
